## Goal
Build a small web app that compares an event's planned expenses with
actual spending and shows the amount remaining or over budget.

## Version 1
- Enter a planned amount for each category.
- Enter actual spending for each category.
- Show the amount remaining or over budget by category.
- Show total planned and actual spending.
- Highlight categories that are over budget.

## Done looks like
1. Changing an actual expense updates its category total.
2. The app calculates planned minus actual spending correctly.
3. Over-budget categories are easy to identify.
4. The page is readable on a phone.
